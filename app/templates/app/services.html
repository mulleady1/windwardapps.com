Custom systems built around how your organization works.

Everything below is built the same way: a small senior team that uses AI heavily, short sprints, and a scope you agree to before work starts.

Custom applications

Web and mobile software shaped around how your organization actually works. Public portals, case and workflow management, field data collection, internal tools, and the integrations that tie them together.

Web is our default because it reaches every device instantly and there is nothing to install or approve. When you need native hardware access or offline use, we build native iOS and Android apps.

Modernization & automation

Paper forms, fax, spreadsheets, and the system nobody wants to touch. We replace them incrementally, keep the data intact, and automate the hand-offs in between so your staff stop re-keying the same information three times.

This kind of work is dramatically cheaper than it used to be, which means modernization projects that never cleared a budget committee are now within reach.

Data & reporting

Pipelines that pull from the systems you already have, reshape the data for the people who need it, and feed dashboards and required reporting. One source of truth, refreshed automatically.

AI integration

We connect the systems you already run to frontier models. Through their APIs, for document intake, summarization and search across your records. Through MCP servers, so an assistant can read from and act on the tools your staff already use. Access controls and audit trails are part of the build, and your data stays yours.

Cloud & operations

We deploy to the cloud, automate the infrastructure, and keep it running. Scale up for peak hours, scale down to save, and call us when something needs attention. You own the accounts and the code.

And the odd stuff

Desktop software, geographic information systems (GIS), Raspberry Pi and other hardware. If it runs code, we've probably built something like it.
