SDVOSB

Windward Apps is a service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) operating under NAICS code 541511, custom computer programming services. We are eligible for SDVOSB set-asides and work as both prime contractor and subcontractor for federal organizations, notably the VA.

State & local

We do business with state and local government agencies in Hawaiʻi and California.

Why agencies work with us

Firm-fixed-price for defined scope, with a short discovery phase first so the scope is real. Time-and-materials for support and for work that is still taking shape. Working software to review within the first sprint, and delivery in months, not years. Because agent-driven development cuts the hours behind every feature, budgets that used to buy a requirements study now buy a finished system.

Every line of code is reviewed by a senior engineer, and your agency owns the code, the data, and the infrastructure accounts from day one.
