Hawaiʻi's AI-augmented software consultancy

A small, senior team in Kailua that builds advanced systems for government and business, faster and for less than the old way allowed.

The what

We build the systems organizations actually run on: custom applications, portals, data pipelines, automation, and the modernization of whatever came before. Our clients are government agencies and businesses in Hawaiʻi and on the mainland, and the work ranges from a single tool for one team to enterprise-scale platforms.

The how

Agent-driven development. Our engineers direct AI agents that write, test and refactor code, then review everything before it ships. That is how a team our size delivers enterprise-scale work in months instead of years, and why our pricing looks the way it does.

The craft hasn't changed: short iterations, working software in your hands early, and a senior engineer accountable for every line. The speed has.

The why

Windward Apps was created to fix the problem of businesses paying too much for software that did too little. Over many years as an employee at various companies, I saw all too often that clients received a subpar result: features missing or built wrong, delivery behind schedule, buggy applications, budgets not respected. I started Windward Apps on one principle: bring excellent customer service to the software service industry. That means software that is elegant, robust, built to spec, well engineered, and delivered on time and on budget. Agent-driven development is the biggest lever I've ever had for keeping that promise. Kyle Mulleady, Founder

Our office

We work out of downtown Kailua on the windward side of Oʻahu, a short walk from Kailua Beach. Come by if you're in the neighborhood.
